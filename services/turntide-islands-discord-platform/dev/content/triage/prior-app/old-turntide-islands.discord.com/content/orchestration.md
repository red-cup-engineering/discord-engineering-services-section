---
title: "Turntide Islands Discord Orchestration"
---

Turntide Islands is a Discord-native community for people seriously talking about climate breakdown without pretending that everyone arrives in a sober, academic, already-composed mode.

The community has two faces:

- A public surface where climate dread, jokes, screenshots, links, and collapse-aware internet culture can enter.
- A member surface where the same material is turned into durable knowledge: briefings, reading paths, vault links, archive exports, and agent-assisted research tasks.

The agentic platform should not farm engagement.
Its job is to notice signals, bring in news and cultural material, ask better questions, rescue useful chat into the vault, and route human-only matters to people.

Revenue is attached to access and maintenance, not to despair.
A paid membership entitlement grants the `islander` role, and that role unlocks member lanes, vault-backed briefings, and agent-assisted retrieval.
Losing payment status is an access event, not a moderation judgment.

Turntide Islands and koios.news are publicly separate projects.
Internally they may share climate-breakdown data stores, source catalogs, vault infrastructure, and agent systems.
Publicly, Turntide should not be described as Koios's Discord, newsroom, funnel, or official community.

The normative carrier for this operating model is exported from `src/index.mts` as `orchestrationProfile`.
